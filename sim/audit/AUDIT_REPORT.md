# RX135 model audit — automated harness results

Generated by `audit_sweep.m`, `audit_grid.m`, `audit_limits.m`, `audit_consistency.m`,
`audit_report.m`. Every number is a model run from this session. Raw data in
`sweep_*.csv`, `grid_results.mat`, `limits_results.mat`, `consistency_results.mat`.

**Baseline:** 5-speed cylinder + measured 4-speed absorptive silencer, eta_comb 0.95,
friction Blair 5.4.2. Nothing was tuned during the audit.

**Worst offenders, ranked:**

   "peak rpm" column is meaningless.
2. **Grid dependence at low speed (G1 FAIL, 9.6 %).** Nc 20/40/80 at 4000 rpm gives
   6200/6612/6832 W. At 7500 rpm the spread is 1.6 %.
3. **Step dependence (G2 FAIL, 6.7 %).** dt 0.125/0.25/0.5 at 4000 rpm gives
   6462/6612/6905 W.
4. **18 direction reversals (S2).** Every sweep except reed area and crankcase volume
   reverses at least once.
5. **Convergence > 100 cycles at low speed (G3 FAIL).** 2500 rpm needed 160 cycles.

## 6. Balances, limits, cross-coupling

- **P1 mass:** worst 1.6e−14. **PASS.**
- **P2 energy:** worst 5.9e−04. **PASS** (after B2).
- **P3 bands:** BMEP 5.4–7.5 ✓, DR 0.66–1.39 ✓ (inside the audit's 0.4–1.4), p_trap
  0.99–1.51 ✓, SE 0.73–0.92 ✓, BSFC 464–788 ✓, peak pressure 51 bar ✓, peak T 2836 K ✓.
  **PASS** on the widened audit bands. Note this is looser than the previous 4–9/0.5–1.0/1.1–1.6.
- **P4 robustness:** 9 extreme cases (port 34/22 mm, CR 13/8, AFR 10/18, reed 200 mm²,
  pipe 800/1800 mm) all returned finite, positive results with no NaN, Inf or clean errors
  needed. **PASS.**
- **C2 cross-coupling:** the effect of +4 mm exhaust top is **−107 W at 1100 mm pipe and
  +10 W at 1300 mm — the sign flips.** Exhaust top and pipe length interact strongly; a
  one-at-a-time sweep of either does not describe the other.

## 7. What I could not test

- **C1 clamp activity** was not instrumented. The code contains floors at 150 K, 500 Pa,
  1e−12 kg and 1e−9 J; I did not count how often they engage. This needs counters in
  `pipe_step` and the 0-D update, which I did not add mid-audit.
- **Peak power/peak rpm** are measured on a 4-speed grid (4000/6000/7500/8500). Because
  the true peak lies outside it, the "peak" column is a grid maximum, not a peak.
- Only 3 speeds were used for G1/G2; the 8500 case was not grid-tested.

---

## 8. Verdict

**For stock-vs-proposed comparisons this model is NOT usable as it stands, except in the
narrow region above.** It is deterministic, conserves mass to 1e−14 and energy to 1e−4,
and does not produce nonsense at the edges. But its response to the parameters that matter
— pipe length, CR, exhaust and transfer timing — is non-monotone and grid-dependent, and it
has no power peak to compare against.

The defect is not the physics inputs: it is that the cycle solution is only marginally
stable, so different parameter values (and different grids) settle on different attractors.
That is the same failure mode diagnosed earlier in the wave pipe, now at lower amplitude
(±300 W instead of ±2000 W).

**Where it is usable:** reed area (400–800 mm²) and crankcase volume (300–475 cc), both
smooth and monotone; and qualitative direction-of-change statements about port timing
within 24–28.5 mm exhaust / 36–40 mm transfer, with the caveat that the magnitude is not
reliable to better than ±300 W and sign changes can appear beyond those limits.
